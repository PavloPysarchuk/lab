# Pavlo's voice & account baseline (@pavlo.ie)

Source: 18 of Pavlo's own Reels transcribed with reel_lab (Sep 2026) + Instagram Insights (last 30 days).

## Who he is on camera
- Results / performance coach. "Hacking the patterns running your life." Helps entrepreneurs do the hard stuff.
- Ukrainian, lives in Ireland; English is fluent but non-native. Slight grammar quirks
  ("why do most people procrastinate", "opposite advices"). Keep scripts in simple, direct English, short
  sentences, but do NOT polish him into a US guru. Natural > perfect.
- Calm, teacherly, reasoning out loud. Explains with props on a table (boxes, scales, objects),
  "Here is the thing", "the way our brain works", "Let it sink."
- Occasional hard swear for emphasis ("What the fuck is wrong with me?"), not constant.
- Speaks FAST: 200-235 wpm on talking heads.
- Uses his own life: 3 years lost in business before learning "make it a must, not an option"
  (story of an entrepreneur who said "get rich or die trying"); client who delayed editing his book for years
  because finishing would kill the fantasy.
- Warm family moments (wife, son) appear in the feed. Real, but off-niche unless tied to a lesson.

## His core ideas (use these as the spine of the content)
1. Pain vs pain: we don't move between pain and pleasure. There's the pain of doing the hard thing and the
   pain of everything staying the same. You move the moment the second one is bigger.
2. People do more to avoid pain than to get pleasure, so stop pumping the dream board and make the cost of staying the same vivid.
3. The carrot for NOT doing: not doing feels good (the fantasy stays alive). Find your carrot.
4. Make it a must, not an option. Discipline isn't the missing piece; available options are.
5. Be your own salesperson: agitate your own pain, show your own heaven, put the offer (action) in between.
6. Internal certainty compensates for missing external certainty (the $1M shopping-centre mind game).
7. 5-step self-talk reset: Thought > Notice > Decode the message > Challenge beliefs > Choose next action.
8. Scrolling = distraction from a task that hurts. Ask why it's still worth doing.
9. "It's not that bad" is how you talk yourself out of change.
10. Stick with one proven strategy long enough; opposite advice is just other people's marketing.

## Implications for scripts
- First line must name a specific person/moment/problem out loud (callout), not a topic or a series name.
- 10-30s for reach pieces; 45-75s only for lead-magnet explainers with a comment keyword.
- Mix formats: POV skits, text+music with the lesson in the caption, talking-head callouts, story-proof.
- Every piece should make an entrepreneur think "that's me". Say founder/business/clients/sales/launch.
