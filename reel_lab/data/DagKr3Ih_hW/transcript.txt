Inner peace is the new rich. Most people want to have cars, and they think about buying a home, and they want to get fancy watches, and they want to have all the bling, and they want to be able to spend money on fancy vacations, and they think they want all these things. But in reality, inner peace is the new rich. Because with all those things, if you are stressed, if you are constantly living in fight or flight mode, if you are overwhelmed, if you don't feel lovable, if you don't feel enough, then all the money in the world, all the fancy things, all the Instagram posts of you in front of fancy cars isn't going to mean squat if you don't feel the peace and harmony within you. The greatest gift you can give yourself is inner peace.
