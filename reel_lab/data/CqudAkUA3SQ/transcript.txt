One of the best laws you could ever apply to your life to increase your happiness is known as the backwards law, as written by philosopher Alan Watts. Now, I love this law, but it is paradoxical and it can play with your mind a little. But what it points at is the more you chase something, the more you try to achieve something, the less likely you are to experience it. So, for example, if you're feeling insecure and you try to achieve something the less likely you are to experience it. So for example if you're feeling insecure and you try to control reality to increase your security you actually increase your insecurity. So what do you do then? Well first of all you have to begin to address the illusion created by your ego around insecurity and there's nothing wrong with creating an intention to be more secure but you drop all attachments to that result actually happening and then the magic begins to happen.
