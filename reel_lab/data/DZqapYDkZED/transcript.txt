Here's 10 years of dating advice in less than one minute. Number one, how somebody treats you in month one is a sales job. How they treat you in month six is who they actually are. Number two, if you're always the one pursuing or always the one being pursued, something is off. Healthy relationships have both people choosing each other consistently. Number three, the best way to date someone amazing is to become someone amazing. Number four, we tend to judge relationships by the peak experiences, the most romantic moments, the greatest nights out, the incredible sex. But the reality of a long-term relationship is your average Tuesday morning. Number five, unhealthy relationships seduce you with fantasy and excitement. Healthy relationships just feel peaceful. Number six, loneliness is a terrible reason to start a relationship, but it's an even worse reason to stay in one. Number seven, if you need them to be a certain way for you to be okay, then you're not okay. Number eight, attraction is not proof. Plenty of people are attracted to things that hurt them. Number nine, someone's potential is not a good reason to stay with them. Love them for who they are now, not for what they one day might become.
