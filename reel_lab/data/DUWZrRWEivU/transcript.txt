They teach NASCAR drivers that if they get in a spin, no matter how close they get to the wall, is if they focus on the wall, what do you think they do? They hit the wall. So what they teach them is if you get in a spin, find a clearing, find an opening between two cars, and stare at that opening no matter what, and try to get there. If you get an inch away from the wall, it doesn't matter. Don't look at it, because if you look at it, you drift, you hit it. Where are you focusing your problems? Or where are you focusing your attention? Are you focusing on the problem, on the wall? What if I put money down and I don't know what I'm doing and I snowball and then I don't have money and then I can't pay the rent? Are you focused on the wall or are you focusing on the solutions and where you want to go, the destination? So many of us are trained to focus on the problems. We think in our minds that the focus on the problem is allowing us to make sure we avoid it, to protect ourselves. When in reality, guys, what we focus on is exactly what we get.
