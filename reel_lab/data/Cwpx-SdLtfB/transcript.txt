What the overthinking, socially anxious person doesn't realize is that they're actually socially intelligent, much more so than the average person. But instead of utilizing that social intelligence to connect with other people, it's directed inward. They're picking up on every nuance of the things that are going on around them, the body language, how this person's emotional state is. And it's all reflecting inward and it's saying, do they like me? Does that mean that I'm good? Am I good enough yet? Do I fit into this environment? And because of the overload of information, you're stifled, you freeze up. You're like, I can't do this, right? But here's the key. If you could just start working through your anxiety, if you started meditating, if you started encountering that fear and letting it go, and you started to become more confident, you'd find that you have a very easy time connecting with others because you can read them so well. It's a superpower disguised as a deficiency. You just have to work through the anxiety.
