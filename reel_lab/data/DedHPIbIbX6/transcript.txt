The hardest habit I coach founders out of isn't procrastination. Procrastination is easier to deal with because you can spot it. This is a thing you've done this morning and yesterday and the day before. This is your favorite kind of work. The work you're really good at and it feels good but it's not currently moving to the middle and you know it. This is what I call productive procrastination. The perfect hiding place because you get to avoid scary things and feel busy doing it. Nobody can call you lazy, not even you.
