I wanna share with you one of the most powerful strategies when you are stuck in a spiral of negative self-talk. Ask yourself this, who told you that? I'm an imposter, who told you that? I'm unworthy, who told you that? There's no point in me trying this thing at work. Who told you that? When we have thoughts, sometimes we treat those thoughts as fact. In other words, we conflate the thought with the thought must be believable, it must be true. When we ask who told you that, you automatically create a sense of distance and separation. You start to observe your thought as a thought, not a fact. To be clear, this is not to deny reality. Rather, it is about not treating your thoughts as if they are immutable. And you might ask yourself, well, what if my parent told me I was unworthy? What if my boss told me that I wasn't good at the job? Again, asking yourself, who told you that, moves yourself into recognizing that the person who told you that is a person with an opinion and what you are hearing is their opinion. Again, it is not a fact. So next time you have a difficult thought, have fun with who told you that. Who told you that? Your dog? Did your dog tell you that? Did Spongebob tell you that? Did Donald Duck tell you that? Who told you that?
