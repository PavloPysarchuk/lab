Hey, I'm Sarah. I'm an executive function coach, which is such a cool job that most people have never heard of. I help busy professionals learn about themselves and figure out systems that actually work for them. My clients often struggle with procrastination, feeling overwhelmed, and feeling stuck because they aren't using the right strategies for them to manage their time, plan, or prioritize. I help you learn about yourself, create realistic plans, and sustainable strategies to reach your goals at work and in your personal life. I also help you prioritize self-care because you deserve that, and I believe that self-care is the foundation of our ability to do all the other things that we need to do. As a coach, I offer support, guidance, and pushes when you need a little extra encouragement to do the hard stuff, and I'm glad you're here.
