The very interesting thing about high performers and self-sabotage is that oftentimes the way in which they are sabotaging is in an unconscious way useful to them. For a lot of high performers, they are motivated by what I call dark motivation or shame-based motivation. There's some rupture that's happened early on in life that ain't to prove themselves to somebody. They've been told that their highest worth or their only value in life and existence is to perform, is to do, and is to perform exceptionally well. And so when that has been your experience, you end up utilizing pain as a fuel source for action. And for a lot of high performers, they have an immense amount of shame that has just never been dealt with. And at some point that always runs out. Where it usually has a net negative impact is the athlete, the entrepreneur have the outcome that they want. They win the award, their music goes platinum, their business blows up. And all of a sudden they're like, I feel fucking empty. They need to do stupid, dumb shit that they feel bad about to have more fuel to then go take more action.
