The paradox of being a high achiever is that 12-year-old you would be so damn proud if they could see you right now. And 80-year-old you would probably laugh at how small you're still playing. I love this frame because it's a beautiful reminder that you can simultaneously hold deep gratitude for where you're at and relentless hunger for where you're going. A heart that says, I know I'm already enough. A head that says, be more anyway. I happen to believe that the art of life is holding both without letting either win. And that tension between the two isn't a problem. That's what gives life meaning. It's the whole fucking point.
