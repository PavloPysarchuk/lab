Success will always be way more about your behaviors than your thoughts. Because a lot of the thoughts I have suck during the day, but I still get what is necessary done. You have to have small daily victories of integrity. It's, you know what, I did some good today. I said I was going to shower and I showered. I said I was going to go to the gym and I went to the gym. I said I was going to call my mom, I called my mom. I said I was going to write the first page and I wrote the first page. And it's these small acts of integrity that improves our thoughts. More than wishing our thoughts were different thoughts.
