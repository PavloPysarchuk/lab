## Visual grammar
Top-left word-by-word captions (next word ghosted, accent word in italic serif + accent colour), mono `// 01 — hook` label + live
HUD counter in the header, one physical metaphor per reel, UI cards with halftone shadows, spring pop-ins, slot-rolling numbers,
stamps, tap cursor, theme flips under halftone dot wipes, presenter gestures on every stressed word. Three house styles: paper
classic (most views), dark minimal with highlighted keyword pills, bold condensed "illustrated".
